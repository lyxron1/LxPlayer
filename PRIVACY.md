# Privacy Policy for LxPlayer

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **LxPlayer** ("we", "our", or "the application") handles user data. LxPlayer is an open-source desktop IPTV and media player designed with a strict **Privacy-First** architecture.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Telemetry
LxPlayer **does not collect, transmit, track, or share any personal data**.
- We do not require account registration or email submission to use the application.
- We do not run any background tracking services, analytics engines (such as Google Analytics or telemetry trackers), or advertising SDKs.
- We do not log or monitor the channels, movies, series, or streams you watch.

---

## 2. Local Storage of User Credentials
- Any IPTV credentials (such as Xtream Codes server URLs, usernames, passwords) or M3U playlist links you enter into the application are stored **exclusively on your local computer** using your system's secure `localStorage` and `IndexedDB`.
- This information **never leaves your machine** and is never sent to our servers, GitHub, or any third party.

---

## 3. Network Communication
- Network requests initiated by LxPlayer are directed **solely to the IPTV or media servers that you explicitly configure**, or to public test streams provided in Demo mode.
- Communication with your IPTV provider is subject to the privacy terms and policies of that respective provider.

---

## 4. Third-Party Codecs & Media Formats
The application uses open-source libraries (such as `Hls.js`) to parse and play standard media formats locally. No personal identifiers or playback telemetry are shared with these libraries.

---

## 5. Contact & Open Source Transparency
Since LxPlayer is an open-source project, the complete source code is publicly accessible and auditable on GitHub:  
👉 **[https://github.com/lyxron1/LxPlayer](https://github.com/lyxron1/LxPlayer)**

For questions or security disclosures, please open an issue on the official GitHub repository.

---

# Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe Özet)

**Geçerlilik Tarihi:** 6 Kasım 2026  

**LxPlayer**, kullanıcı gizliliğini temel ilke olarak benimsemiş açık kaynaklı bir masaüstü medya oynatıcısıdır.

1. **Sıfır Veri Toplama:** Uygulama hiçbir kişisel bilginizi, IP adresinizi, izleme geçmişinizi veya kullanım verinizi toplamaz, kaydetmez veya üçüncü taraflarla paylaşmaz.
2. **Yerel Depolama:** Eklediğiniz Xtream / M3U kullanıcı bilgileri yalnızca kendi bilgisayarınızın yerel depolama alanında saklanır; dış sunuculara veya geliştiriciye gönderilmez.
3. **Ağ İletişimi:** Oynatıcı yalnızca sizin tanımladığınız IPTV sunucularıyla doğrudan iletişim kurar.
4. **Şeffaflık:** Kod tabanı herkes tarafından denetlenebilir şekilde GitHub üzerinde açıktır.
